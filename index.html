Privacy Policy for BondhuGo

Last Updated: October 24, 2024

Thank you for choosing BondhuGo ("we," "us," "our"). This Privacy Policy is designed to help you understand how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

By using BondhuGo, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

To provide and improve our service, we collect the following types of information:

a. Information You Provide

  • Profile Information: When you create your profile, you provide us with your name, a unique public username, date of birth, and gender. You can also add an optional bio and profile picture.
  • Public Content: We collect content you submit to public areas of the app, such as posts (including text, photos, and videos) and comments.
  • Private Communications: We facilitate the delivery of private messages between users. As these messages are end-to-end encrypted (see Section 3), we cannot read their content. We also collect media you send in chats, like GIFs and voice messages.
  • Saved Content: We store a list of posts you save to make them accessible to you later.

b. Information Collected Automatically

  • Google Sign-In: When you sign up using Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from your Google account to create your BondhuGo profile.
  • Push Notification Token: We collect a unique token (FCM Token) for your device to send you push notifications about important activities, such as new messages and friend requests.
  • Usage and Device Data: We use Firebase services, which may automatically collect technical data like device information, crash reports, and performance metrics to help us improve our service and fix bugs.

c. Payment Information

  • Premium Services: If you purchase a premium subscription, your payment information is typically collected and processed directly by our payment partner, **Razorpay**. **Please note that Razorpay integration is currently disabled and subscription purchases are unavailable. This will be addressed in a subsequent application update.** We do not receive or store your full credit card or bank account details.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Provide and Maintain the Service: To create your account, deliver private messages, display your profile and public content, and enable app functionalities.
  • Communicate with You: To send you push notifications about new messages, friend requests, and other in-app activities.
  • Personalize Your Experience: To provide features like Saved Posts and show content relevant to your interests.
  • Process Payments: To manage subscriptions and access to premium features through our payment partner (when available).
  • Ensure Security: To protect your account and our platform from fraud and unauthorized access.
  • Improve Our App: To analyze usage data, fix bugs, and develop new features.
Important Service Note: We are aware of a problem currently affecting the reliability of the push notification system. This issue will be resolved and fixed in a subsequent application update.

3. Data Storage and Security

a. End-to-End Encryption (E2EE)

Your privacy is our priority. All one-to-one private chat messages on BondhuGo are end-to-end encrypted. This means that only you and the person you are communicating with can read what is sent. No one in between, not even BondhuGo, can decrypt and read your messages.

b. Data Storage

  • Your profile information, public posts, and other metadata are securely stored on Google Cloud Firestore.
  • Media files you upload, such as profile pictures, images, videos, and voice messages, are stored on Firebase Storage.
  • Your search history is stored only on your local device and is not sent to our servers.

c. Data Security

We implement strong security measures, including Firestore Security Rules, to protect your data from unauthorized access. You have full control to edit your profile and delete your own content.

4. Third-Party Services

We rely on the following trusted third-party services, which have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Sign-In, Firebase, Google Cloud: Google Privacy Policy
  • Giphy: To provide GIFs in chat. Giphy Privacy Policy
  • Razorpay: For processing payments. **Note: This service is currently disabled.** Razorpay Privacy Policy

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party services.

5. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to access and control your personal data.

  • Access and Update: You can view and edit your profile information at any time through the app settings.
  • Data Deletion: You can delete your own posts and comments. You can also permanently delete your entire account and associated data directly from the app's settings screen.

6. Children's Privacy

BondhuGo is not intended for use by children under the age of **16**. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under **16**.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy within the app or on our website. You are advised to review this policy periodically.
