Privacy Policy for BondhuGo

Last Updated: March 10, 2026

Thank you for using BondhuGo ("we," "us," "our"). This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application. By using BondhuGo, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

a. Information You Provide Directly

  • Profile Information: When you register, you provide your name, a unique public username, and date of birth. You can also upload a profile picture.
  • Public Content: We collect content you post publicly, which includes text and images.
  • Private Communications: We handle the delivery of private messages, including text and GIFs. To enhance user privacy, we have implemented a **Message Request** system for new chats.
  • Block List: To ensure a safe community, we store a list of users you have blocked.

b. Information Collected Automatically

  • Google Sign-In: We use your Google account for authentication, from which we receive your name, email address, and profile picture to pre-fill your profile.
  • Push Notification Token: We use **OneSignal** to collect a unique token for your device to send you push notifications for new messages and other important activities.
  • Online Presence: We use **Firebase Realtime Database** to show your "online" or "offline" status to other users in real-time.
  • Usage Data: Firebase services automatically collect technical data like device info and crash reports to help us improve the app.

c. Locally Stored Data (On Your Device)

  • Cache Memory: To provide a "butter smooth" offline experience, we use **Hive** to cache your posts, chat messages, and search history directly on your device. This data is not sent to our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To Provide the Service: To create and maintain your account, deliver messages, display your content, and enable app functionalities like the Message Request and Block systems.
  • To Improve User Experience: To provide a faster experience by loading cached data and to fix bugs using performance metrics.
  • To Communicate With You: To send important notifications about new messages and app updates via OneSignal.
  • To Ensure Security: To protect our platform from spam and to enforce blocking rules.

3. Data Storage and Security

  • Your profile information, posts, and chat metadata are stored on **Google Cloud Firestore**.
  • Images you upload (for posts and your profile) are stored on **Cloudinary**.
  • Your online presence status is managed through **Firebase Realtime Database**.
  • We implement strong security measures, including Firestore Security Rules, to protect your data.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following trusted third-party services:

  • Google (Firebase, Google Sign-In): For authentication, database, and analytics. Google Privacy Policy
  • OneSignal: For push notifications. OneSignal Privacy Policy
  • Cloudinary: For image hosting and delivery. Cloudinary Privacy Policy
  • Giphy: For providing GIFs in chat. Giphy Privacy Policy

5. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access and Update: You can view and edit your profile information (name, date of birth, profile picture) anytime from the app's settings screen.
  • Content Control: You can delete your own posts. You can also block other users to prevent them from interacting with you.
  • Account Deletion: You can permanently delete your account from the app settings.

6. Children's Privacy

BondhuGo is not intended for individuals under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy within the app. You are advised to review this page periodically.
